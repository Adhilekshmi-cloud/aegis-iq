1. System Overview

The Aegis Series-7 is a 3000 PSI class dual-redundant hydraulic flight control system designed for high-performance aerodynamic surface positioning and flight control stabilization. The system provides continuous hydraulic power to flight-critical actuators under dynamic aerodynamic load regimes while maintaining stringent fault-tolerant isolation across power channels.

2. System Architecture & Redundancy

The architecture employs a fully segregated, symmetrical dual-channel configuration comprising a Left Hydraulic Channel and a Right Hydraulic Channel. Each channel maintains dedicated mechanical power takeoff, fluid storage, conditioning, and distribution circuits:

  • Left Hydraulic Channel: Powered by Primary Engine Drive #1 via pump PMP-5000 (Left), feeding the left manifold and primary actuator channel A.
  • Right Hydraulic Channel: Powered by Primary Engine Drive #2 via pump PMP-5000 (Right), feeding the right manifold and primary actuator channel B.
  • Redundancy & Automatic Failover: Dual-channel with automatic failover, each channel independently powers flight control surfaces. In the event of channel depressurization, hydraulic isolation valves isolate the degraded branch in less than 15 milliseconds, transferring full authority to the active channel without loss of flight surface stiffness or transient disturbance.

6. Key Component Architecture Descriptions

PA-7100 Primary Actuator

The Primary Actuator PA-7100 serves as the direct mechanical interface to the primary aerodynamic control surfaces. Built from forged Titanium Ti-6Al-4V, it utilizes dual tandem hydraulic cylinders powered by independent channel lines to guarantee full mechanical authority upon single-channel depressurization.

SV-4200 Electrohydraulic Servo Valve

The SV-4200 electrohydraulic servo valve regulates high-pressure fluid directly into the PA-7100 chambers. Operating on pilot stage torque-motor deflection, it features precision-machined 17-4PH stainless steel spool sleeves rated for 15 GPM nominal flow with an 8 ms step response.

PMP-5000 Primary Hydraulic Pump

The PMP-5000 is an engine-driven variable displacement pump constructed with Inconel 718 rotating groups. It self-regulates displacement up to 28.5 cc/rev to maintain nominal circuit pressure across speed variations between 800 and 4200 RPM.

ACC-1800 Hydraulic Accumulator & Conditioning

Pulsation damping and emergency transient reserve are provided by the ACC-1800 bladder accumulator, precharged with dry nitrogen to 1500 PSI at 20°C. Downstream fluid clarity is secured via the FLT-2100 10-micron filter assembly with mechanical bypass activation at 75 PSI differential.
