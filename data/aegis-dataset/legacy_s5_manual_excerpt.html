CRITICAL NOTICE: This manual describes legacy Series-5 hydraulic equipment. Series-5 hydraulic systems and sub-components are fundamentally incompatible with Series-7 architectures. Do not cross-reference pressure specifications, filtration consumables, or hydraulic fluids between Series-5 and Series-7.

1. System Architecture Overview (Series-5)

The Aegis Series-5 Hydraulic Power System is a centralized, single-channel hydraulic network engineered for utility actuation and mechanical primary flight surfaces. Unlike subsequent dual-redundant designs, the Series-5 operates with a single continuous pressure distribution line supplemented by a passive emergency blow-down reserve.

2. Fluid and Operating Parameters

Parameter Specification Value Notes
Hydraulic Fluid Type MIL-H-5606 Mineral-based hydraulic fluid FLAMMABLE. Incompatible with polyol ester fluids (MIL-PRF-83282). Mixing causes elastomer swell and seal destruction.
Nominal Operating Pressure 2500 PSI Regulated via mechanical unloader valve. Maximum transient overshoot 2750 PSI.
Proof Pressure 3125 PSI 1.25x design limit
Burst Pressure 3750 PSI 1.50x design limit
System Architecture Single-channel simplex Zero automated cross-channel failover; manual emergency gear release only
Nominal Flow Rate 10.5 GPM Single engine accessory drive gearbox pump

3. Legacy Component Designations

The following line replaceable units (LRUs) are specific to the Series-5 architecture:

  • PA-5100 Actuator: Simplex linear actuator with internal mechanical locking mechanism. Operates strictly at 2500 PSI nominal pressure.
  • SV-3100 Servo Valve: Single-coil flapper-nozzle electrohydraulic valve. Max rated flow 10 GPM at 1000 PSI drop.
  • PMP-3500 Pump: Fixed-displacement axial piston pump rated for 2500 PSI continuous output.
  • FLT-1200 Filter: 15-micron cellulose disposable filter cartridge.

4. Decommissioning & Transition Note

All airframes transitioned to the Series-7 architecture (S/N 1001 and higher) utilize dual-channel redundancy, MIL-PRF-83282 synthetic fluid, and 3000+ PSI pressure levels. Series-5 parts (PA-5100, SV-3100, PMP-3500) must never be installed on Series-7 aircraft.
